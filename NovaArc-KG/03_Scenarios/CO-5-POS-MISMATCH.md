# 🩺 Hospital-Only Procedure Billed with Office POS (11) or Vice Versa

**CARC Code:** [[CO-5]]  
**Category:** [[Coding, Inconsistencies & Modifier Edits]]  
**Root Cause:** CPT code can only be performed in inpatient facility (POS 21) or ambulatory surgical center (POS 24), but was billed with POS 11 (Office).
